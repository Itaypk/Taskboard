{{appName}}

A personal AI weekly planner. Capture tasks. Commit to a week. Let the assistant schedule it.

What it does

{{appName}} is a personal planner that turns your task backlog into a realistic weekly plan – using tried-and-true methods. Once a week you have a short planning conversation with an AI assistant, on Telegram or on the web. The assistant asks what you want to get done, proposes a plan that fits your week, and sends the agreed tasks to your calendar as time-block invitations.

How it works

  1. Capture tasks as they come up.
  2. Plan your week in a quick chat – the assistant proposes a plan based on your priorities and availability.
  3. Commit to it – agreed tasks are scheduled as time blocks on your calendar.
  4. Follow up and adjust – didn't get it this time? Unfinished tasks never go missing.
