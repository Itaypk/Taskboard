Backlog.fyi

An AI-powered weekly planner. Pin up your tasks. Let the assistant schedule your week.

What it does

Backlog.fyi is a hobby, non-commercial weekly planner. You capture tasks on a digital cork board as you think of them. Once a week you have a short planning conversation with an AI assistant on Telegram. The assistant reads your Google Calendar, asks what you want to get done, and writes the agreed tasks back into your calendar as time blocks.

How it works

  1. Capture tasks in the backlog whenever they come up.
  2. Each week, the assistant messages you on Telegram to plan.
  3. It reads your Google Calendar and schedules the tasks you agree on.

Status

The backlog (tasks, categories, tags) is live. The Telegram weekly planning conversation and Google Calendar integration are in active development.
