Backlog.fyi

A personal AI weekly planner. Capture tasks. Commit to a week. Let the assistant schedule it.

What it does

Backlog.fyi is a personal planner that turns your task backlog into a realistic weekly plan – using tried-and-true methods. Once a week you have a short planning conversation with an AI assistant on Telegram. The assistant reads your Google Calendar, asks what you want to get done, and writes the agreed tasks back into your calendar as time blocks.

How it works

  1. Capture tasks as they come up.
  2. Plan your week in a quick chat – the assistant proposes a plan based on your priorities and availability.
  3. Commit to it – agreed tasks are scheduled as time blocks on your calendar.
  4. Follow up and adjust – didn't get it this time? Unfinished tasks never go missing.

Status

The Backlog.fyi application is a work in progress. Some features are incomplete, and some aspects might change through the development process.
