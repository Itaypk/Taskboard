## What it is

A weekly planner built around two ideas:

* **Capture is cheap.** Tasks should land in a backlog the moment you think of them — from the web
  app, from a Telegram message, from a script or an AI assistant through the API.
* **Committing is the hard part.** Once a week you sit down with an assistant that knows your
  backlog, and you agree on what actually fits in the week ahead. Whatever you agree to goes out
  as calendar invitations, so it lands as real time in your week.

Everything else is in service of those two.

## Status

Backlog.fyi is in **beta**. It runs on a single server and serves a small group of early users.
Things may break, features may change, and occasional short downtime is part of the deal. If that's
not what you're after, this probably isn't the right tool for you yet.

Task titles, descriptions and other private content are encrypted at rest. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the details and the [Terms of Service](/terms) for the rest.
