## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Backlog.fyi collects, uses, and handles your data.

This is a small, non-commercial project. The goal is to collect as little data as possible and use it only to provide the service.

## 2. Data Collected

The service may collect and store:

* Account information (e.g., email, identifiers)
* Tasks, notes, and preferences
* Calendar data (if you connect a calendar)
* Messages sent via Telegram or email integrations
* Basic technical data (e.g., logs, usage, error reports)

## 3. How Data Is Used

Your data is used only to:

* Provide and operate the service
* Enable AI features
* Sync with connected services (Telegram, email, calendar)
* Monitor and maintain system health
* Prevent abuse

## 4. Data Ownership and Control

You retain full ownership of your data.

You can:

* Request a full export of your data
* Delete your account and associated data at any time

After deletion, some data (such as logs and backups) may remain for up to 30 days before being permanently removed.

## 5. Data Sharing

Your data is not sold.

Your data is not shared with third parties except:

* When required by law
* When necessary to operate the service (see section 6)

## 6. Third-Party Services

The service relies on third-party providers that may process or have access to your data. These include (but are not limited to):

* Telegram (messaging)
* AI providers via OpenRouter (AI processing)
* Email providers (sending emails)
* Grafana Cloud (logs and metrics)
* CDNs (static assets like libraries and fonts)

These providers have their own privacy policies and practices.

While reasonable effort is made to choose privacy-respecting providers and configurations, there is no guarantee that they will not store or use data outside of this service’s control.

## 7. AI Processing

When you use AI features:

* Your input may be sent to third-party AI providers
* Responses are generated based on that input

AI systems may produce incorrect or unexpected outputs.

## 8. Logs and Monitoring

Basic logs and metrics are collected to operate and improve the service.

This may include:

* Error logs
* Usage patterns
* Performance data

Logs are retained for up to 30 days.

## 9. Security

Reasonable effort is made to protect your data.

Sensitive user content — such as task titles, descriptions, notes, and integration messages — is encrypted at rest.

However, no system is completely secure, and no guarantees are made regarding absolute security.

## 10. Data Retention

* Active account data is stored as long as your account exists
* Logs and backups may be retained for up to 30 days

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The service is not intended for users under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your country).

## 12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change at any time.

Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.

## 13. Contact

For any questions, data requests, or deletion requests, contact the project owner.
