# Trademarks and branding

"Backlog.fyi" and the Backlog.fyi logo and app icons (together, the "Marks") belong to Itay
Polack-Gadassi. The software is free and open source, but the Marks are not part of what the
licenses give you. This is a limited exception the AGPL expressly allows (section 7(e), declining to
grant trademark rights), and CC BY 4.0 says the same (section 2(b)(2)).

The rules are deliberately light. The one thing this is here to prevent is a competing public
service that looks like the official one.

## What you may do

- **Self-host it, modified or not**, for yourself, your family, your team or your organisation,
  keeping the bundled name, logo and icons.
- **Refer to it factually**: "based on Backlog.fyi", "works with Backlog.fyi", a link to this
  repository, screenshots in an article or a review.

## What you may not do

- Use the logo or app icons for a **service you offer to the public** (free or paid).
- Present your deployment, fork or service as the official Backlog.fyi, or as endorsed by it.

## If you offer a public service

Replace the files listed under "Branding" in [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) (favicons, app icons, social
image) with your own, and give the service a name that doesn't pass it off as the official one.

The mascot artwork is a different matter: it is under CC BY 4.0 and can be reused with credit.
