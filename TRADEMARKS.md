# Trademarks and branding

"Backlog.fyi", the Backlog.fyi logo and app icons, and the `backlog.fyi` domain name (together, the
"Marks") belong to Itay Polack-Gadassi. The software is free and open source, but the Marks are not
part of what the licenses give you. This is a limited exception the AGPL expressly allows (section
7(e), declining to grant trademark rights), and CC BY 4.0 says the same (section 2(b)(2)).

The goal is simple: you can self-host Backlog.fyi as it is, without designing your own icons, but
nobody should be able to pass off a different service or a modified version as the official one.

## What you may do

- **Self-host an unmodified copy** for yourself, your family, your team or your organisation, with
  the bundled name and icons left as they are.
- **Refer to it factually**: "based on Backlog.fyi", "works with Backlog.fyi", a link to this
  repository, screenshots in an article or a review.

## What you may not do

- Use the Marks as the name or logo of a **modified version, a fork, or a service you offer to the
  public** (free or paid), or in any other way that could make people think it is the official
  service or is endorsed by it.
- Register domain names, social media handles, app-store listings or company names that contain
  the Marks or something confusingly similar.

## If you fork or offer a modified version

Give it its own name and replace the branding:

- the files listed under "Branding" in [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) (favicons, app icons, social image);
- the visible name in the UI strings (`tasker-frontend/src/locales/*/translation.json`) and in the
  email templates and message bundles under `src/main/resources/`;
- and say clearly that your version is unofficial.

The mascot artwork is a different matter: it is under CC BY 4.0 and can be reused with credit.
